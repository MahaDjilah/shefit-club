Club Spaces

Welcome to SheFit, your new favorite gym club designed just for you. A place where women can freely enjoy their favorite workouts in a warm, supportive atmosphere with top-quality spaces. Explore our facilities and start your journey today!

Weights Area

A fully equipped strength-training zone featuring dumbbells, barbells, kettlebells, and resistance machines. Perfect for building muscle, toning your body, and boosting overall strength. Designed with plenty of space and proper equipment to ensure a safe and effective workout for women of all fitness levels.


See Strength Classes

Cardio Room

A fully equipped space with treadmills, ellipticals, stationary bikes, and rowing machines. Perfect for burning calories, building endurance, and keeping your heart strong. Designed to keep you motivated with an energetic atmosphere and music that moves you.


See Cardio Classes

Pilates Studio

A serene studio dedicated to Pilates and core-strength workouts. With mats, reformers, and resistance tools, this space helps improve flexibility, posture, balance, and overall body tone in a calm and supportive environment.


Join Pilates Classes

Swimming Pool

Our indoor swimming pool offers both lap lanes and leisure areas. Ideal for cardiovascular fitness, low-impact training, and full-body conditioning. Aqua aerobics classes are also available for a fun, joint-friendly workout.


View Swim Classes

Kickboxing Zone

High-energy zone equipped with punching bags, mats, and pads for kickboxing and martial arts-inspired workouts. Build strength, stamina, and confidence while learning powerful movements in a supportive environment.


Try Kickboxing

Paddle Area

Enjoy racquet and paddle sports in a dedicated space that improves coordination, agility, and reflexes. A fun, social area perfect for friendly matches or skill-building classes.


Book Paddle Class

Pre/Postnatal Fitness Room

A safe, specialized space for moms-to-be and new mothers. Offers gentle strength, mobility, and cardio exercises tailored to support pregnancy and postpartum recovery, with guidance from experienced trainers.


Join Mom Fitness

Locker & Shower Area

Spacious, secure locker rooms with private showers and changing areas. Relax and refresh before or after your workout, with amenities designed for comfort and convenience.


Explore Amenities

Fit Bar

Our on-site Fit Bar serves healthy snacks, protein shakes, energy drinks, and post-workout recovery options. A perfect stop to refuel, socialize, or grab quick nutrition after training.
